### **React Native App Overview for Empowering The Nation**

The **React Native app** for Empowering The Nation mirrors the functionality of the website, designed to provide users with a mobile-friendly way to browse and enroll in courses. Below are the primary functions and structure of the app:

### **1. Home Screen:**
   - The app opens to a welcoming **Home Screen** with the company logo at the top, a brief introduction to the company, and navigation buttons for course categories.
   - Users can choose between viewing **Six-Week Courses** and **Six-Month Courses**.

### **2. Six-Week Courses Screen:**
   - This screen displays a list of six-week courses available for selection. Courses like **Child Minding**, **Cooking**, and **Garden Maintenance** are listed along with their fees.
   - Each course is presented with a checkbox to allow users to select multiple courses.
   - Once courses are selected, users can navigate to the cart to review their selections.

### **3. Six-Month Courses Screen:**
   - Similar to the six-week screen, the **Six-Month Courses Screen** presents long-term courses such as **Advanced Child Care**, **Professional Cooking**, and **Landscaping & Garden Design**.
   - Users can select any of these courses by checking the boxes next to the course details.

### **4. Cart Screen:**
   - The **Cart Screen** summarizes the selected courses from both the six-week and six-month categories.
   - It calculates the total fee and applies discounts based on the number of courses selected:
     - **1 Course**: No discount.
     - **2 Courses**: 5% discount.
     - **3 Courses**: 10% discount.
     - **More than 3 Courses**: 15% discount.
   - The cart displays the discounted total and provides a button to proceed to the payment screen.

### **5. Payment Screen:**
   - Users are directed to the **Payment Screen** after reviewing their cart.
   - This screen allows users to securely process their payments and confirm enrollment in the selected courses.

### **Key Features of the App:**
1. **Modern Design**: The app uses a sleek blue and grey color palette to maintain a professional and modern look.
2. **Responsive Layout**: The app is designed to be fully responsive, ensuring that all elements are easy to interact with on mobile devices.
3. **User-Friendly Navigation**: Intuitive buttons and checkboxes make it easy for users to select courses, review their cart, and proceed with payment.
4. **Discount Calculation**: The app automatically applies discounts based on the number of courses selected, providing users with a clear summary of the total cost.
5. **Image Placeholders**: Placeholder images are used for courses and logos, making it easy to replace with actual assets later.

The React Native app provides a smooth, user-friendly experience for customers who prefer using mobile devices to browse, select, and enroll in courses offered by Empowering The Nation.
